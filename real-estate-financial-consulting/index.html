Common Questions

Real Estate Financial Consulting — Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from your underwriting service?
−
Underwriting focuses on evaluating a deal before you buy it. Real estate financial consulting supports the ownership and operating period after — ongoing reporting, budgeting, and lender relationships.
Can you manage reporting across a multi-property portfolio?
+
Yes — consolidated, portfolio-level reporting across multiple properties or entities is a core part of this service.
Do you work directly with our lenders or investors?
+
Yes, when helpful. We can prepare and, when appropriate, directly deliver reporting packages to lenders or investor partners on your behalf.
Can you support an active development or construction project?
+
Yes — tracking development and construction budgets against the original pro forma, and flagging variances early, is a common part of this engagement.
Do you work with real estate owners outside of Florida?
+
Yes. We work with real estate owners and operators across the United States, with site visits arranged as needed.
