Is It Time for Dedicated FP&A Support?

If planning and forecasting still live in a fragile spreadsheet only one person understands, it’s time for a real model.

  • ✓ Your budget is built once a year and never revisited against actuals.
  • ✓ Your model breaks or needs a rebuild every time an assumption changes.

Common Questions

FP&A & Financial Modeling — Frequently Asked Questions

Will you build the model in Excel or Google Sheets?
−
Whichever your team already works in — most commonly Excel or Google Sheets. We build models to be owned and updated by you, not a black box only we can maintain.
Can you rebuild a model we already have?
+
Yes. A common starting point is auditing an existing model, fixing broken logic or fragile assumptions, and rebuilding it into something your team can trust and update going forward.
Do you help present the model to investors or our board?
+
Yes — we can help build the supporting narrative and materials, and are available to join the meeting to answer detailed financial questions directly.
How often should a financial model be updated?
+
Most businesses benefit from a monthly refresh against actuals, with a full reforecast quarterly or whenever a major assumption changes (new financing, a new product line, a market shift).
Do you work with businesses outside of Florida?
+
Yes. We work with clients across the United States. FP&A and modeling work is well suited to remote collaboration.
