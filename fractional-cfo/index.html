Signs It's Time

When Should You Hire a Fractional CFO?

Most businesses bring on a fractional CFO once they've outgrown what a bookkeeper or part-time accountant can provide. Common signs it's time:

  • ✓ You're raising capital or taking on debt and need a defensible financial model behind you
  • ✓ You don't have real visibility into cash flow, or where margin is being lost
  • ✓ You're making pricing, expansion, or acquisition decisions without financial modeling to support them
  • ✓ Your board, investors, or lenders expect professional-grade reporting you can't currently produce
  • ✓ You've outgrown your bookkeeper, but a full-time CFO isn't the right cost for your stage yet

Common Questions

Fractional CFO: FAQ

What is a fractional CFO?
−
A fractional CFO is an experienced finance executive who works with your business part-time or on retainer, providing the strategic financial leadership of a full-time CFO — board reporting, capital planning, forecasting — without the cost of a full-time hire.
When should I hire a fractional CFO?
+
Most businesses bring on a fractional CFO when they've outgrown what a bookkeeper can provide: when they need real forecasting and cash flow visibility, are raising capital or taking on debt, or are making decisions that need financial modeling behind them.
How much does a fractional CFO cost?
+
Nationally, fractional CFO engagements typically run $3,000–$15,000 per month depending on scope and hours, or $175–$450 per hour for project-based work — a fraction of a full-time CFO's salary and benefits. F&M Management scopes and prices each engagement to the specific business, so book a call for a proposal tailored to you.
Do I need a CFO or a Controller?
+
A Controller owns accurate, timely accounting — the monthly close, reconciliations, and financial reporting. A CFO owns strategy — forecasting, capital planning, and using that reporting to guide decisions. Many growing businesses need both, which is why fractional engagements often combine the two.
Do you work with businesses outside of Florida?
+
Yes. We work with growing businesses across the United States. Most engagements are run remotely, with in-person availability when it’s useful.
