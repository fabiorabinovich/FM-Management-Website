Signs It's Time

Is It Time for a Startup CFO?

Early-stage finance needs are different from an established business. Here’s when dedicated CFO support starts to pay for itself.

  • ✓ You’re raising a round and need investor-ready financials and a defensible model.
  • ✓ You don’t have a confident, current answer to "what’s our runway."
  • ✓ Your cap table, burn rate, and unit economics live in a spreadsheet only the founder understands.
  • ✓ Your board or investors are asking for reporting you don’t currently produce.
  • ✓ You’re making hiring or spending decisions without a clear financial plan behind them.

Common Questions

Startup CFO Services — Frequently Asked Questions

At what stage should a startup bring on a fractional CFO?
−
Most founders bring in fractional CFO support around their seed to Series A stage — when fundraising, board reporting, or basic financial infrastructure start to outgrow what a founder or bookkeeper can manage alone.
Can you help us prepare for a fundraise?
+
Yes — this is one of the most common reasons startups engage us, including building the financial model, preparing data room materials, and supporting investor diligence questions.
Do you replace the need for a full-time finance hire?
+
For many early-stage startups, yes, at least initially. As the company scales, we often help design and hire the in-house finance team, and can stay on in an oversight or CFO-advisory capacity.
How involved will you be day-to-day?
+
That’s scoped to your needs — some startups want a few hours a week of strategic support, others want deeper weekly involvement through a fundraise or a major growth phase.
Do you work with startups outside of Florida?
+
Yes. We work with startups across the United States. Startup CFO engagements are well suited to remote collaboration, with travel available for board meetings or investor meetings as needed.
